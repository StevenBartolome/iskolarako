Design System · IskolarAko Student App

Visual Metaphor

Official Certificates & Verification Seals
Carry a ledger/document aesthetic throughout — border patterns, seal stamps, receipt-style transaction layouts, parchment tones — balanced with a warm, encouraging student voice.

Typography Pairing

Playfair Display
Display / headings · Serif — conveys credibility and authority
Inter Regular · Medium
Body / UI labels · Geometric sans — clean and legible

Key Design Principles

✦ Warm parchment background — never cold white
✦ DM Mono for all numbers, hashes, refs — signals precision
✦ Blockchain shown as a receipt, never as crypto UI
✦ PayMongo surfaces only as "funds received" — no payment flow
✦ Status tracked on a clear vertical timeline — reassurance over control
✦ Green forest = trust · Amber = attention · Gold = celebration
